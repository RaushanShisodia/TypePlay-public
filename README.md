# TypePlay: Free Typing Lessons, Typing Tests and Typing Games

**TypePlay ([typeplay.org](https://typeplay.org)) is a free typing school for kids and adults: 111 step-by-step
touch-typing lessons, typing tests and 17 typing games. No account or sign-up needed.**

## What you can do on TypePlay

- **Learn** to touch type with [111 guided lessons in 9 levels](https://typeplay.org/typing-learn): home row first, a few
  keys at a time, with an on-screen keyboard and a hand guide that shows which finger presses each key.
- **Practise** with [words, timed runs, quotes, numbers and code](https://typeplay.org/typing-practice), with live WPM
  and accuracy.
- **Test** your speed with a [1-minute typing test](https://typeplay.org/typing-test/timed?t=60) and
  [more typing tests](https://typeplay.org/typing-test), then get a
  [free typing certificate](https://typeplay.org/typing-certificate).
- **Play** [17 typing games](https://typeplay.org/typing-games), from gentle kids' games to an arcade.
- **Track** progress with stars, streaks, badges and a parents' report, saved in your own browser.

No account, no email, nothing to install. The lessons and the kids' games have no ads.

## Meet PlayBuddy and PlayType

**PlayBuddy** (the boy in the blue hoodie) is TypePlay's typing coach: he introduces Practice and the typing tests.
**PlayType** (the girl in the pink hoodie and bow) is the learning friend: she introduces the lessons, games and stats.
They star in TypePlay's games and cheer learners on through the course.
[Read more about PlayBuddy and PlayType](https://typeplay.org/blog/meet-playbuddy-and-playtype).
